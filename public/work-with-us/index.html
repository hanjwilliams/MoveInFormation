Open Position: Student Assistant (HiWi)

MoveinFormation Lab, Department of Biology & CASCB, University of Konstanz 15 October – 15 December 2026

We are looking for a student assistant to support our collective bio-logging work on avian species. Tasks include:

  1. Assembling and maintaining bio-logging field kits before and after deployment
  2. Supporting field logistics planning for our Argentina campaign
  3. Joining the team in the field in Argentina for bio-logging deployments
  4. Organising multiple bio-logging data types post field campaign

Open to undergraduate and Master’s students enrolled at the University of Konstanz.

To apply, contact hannah.williams@uni-konstanz.de

We are always interested in hearing from motivated researchers and students working at the interface of behavioural and movement ecology, environmental physics, and predictive modelling. If you are interested in a research project, Master’s thesis, PhD position, postdoctoral fellowship, or collaboration, please get in touch — we are happy to discuss ideas and funding routes.
