Data collected by the Move.inFormation group contribute to advancing open and reproducible movement ecology research. Our projects generate high-resolution datasets from eye-tracking, bio-logging sensors, GPS tracking, and environmental monitoring systems.
Where possible, data are shared through established repositories and collaborative platforms to support transparency, reuse, and new scientific discoveries.
