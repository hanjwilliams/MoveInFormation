## Air–Surface Interface Swarm Robotics

Pranav is a biologist and roboticist whose research translates principles of collective behaviour observed in animal systems into autonomous robotic platforms. Focusing on swarm robotics operating at the air–surface interface, he develops distributed systems capable of sensing and responding to dynamic environmental conditions. Drawing inspiration from biological movement and collective sensing, his work investigates how shared information can improve navigation and decision-making in uncertain environments. Outside the lab, he is also a keen sailor, bringing practical experience with wind-driven movement systems into his research perspective.
