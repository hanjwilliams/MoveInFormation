## Smart Biologging Technology for the Tracking of Animal Collectives

Timm is a software engineer developing next-generation biologging technologies designed to measure behaviour and interactions within animal collectives. His work focuses on designing intelligent sensor systems that minimise device size and energy demands while maximising the behavioural and environmental insights that can be extracted from the data they collect. By enabling multiple animals to function as distributed sensing networks, his research expands the ability of movement ecology to study collective behaviour and environmental interactions in the wild.
